Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

BallIdentifier ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what happens when you use the bot, website, and related services.

2. Data We Collect

User Data: When you use BallIdentifier, we may collect and store the following information:

Data Type Description
Discord User ID Your unique Discord identifier for authentication and tracking
Username Your Discord username for logging and support purposes
Identification Count Number of successful identifications attributed to your account
Notification Preferences Your optional settings for thank-you messages and upvote reminder DMs
Upvote Cache A local cache of current upvoters fetched from Discord Bot List
Operational Logs Command names, usernames, guild names, and error details needed to operate and debug the service
Ballsdex Link (optional) If you connect your Ballsdex account with Sign in with Ballsdex: the access and refresh tokens Ballsdex gives BallIdentifier (read-only access, stored encrypted), and the data synced with them (a collection summary such as counts, catch times and rankings, plus your special-variant records). Accounts linked earlier with a Ballsdex API key: that key, stored encrypted, until you unlink or sign in again
Collectors If you add yourself as a collector on the website: your Discord ID, the dex and ball, and when you added it. For everyone listed as a collector, the bot caches the public Discord display name, username and avatar URL (refreshed weekly) to show the list
Public Profile (optional) If you create a profile on the website: your bio, pronouns, accent color, favorite dex, showcase balls and visibility settings, plus your Discord display name, username and avatar URL. It stays private until you turn on Public profile; then anyone can see it at ballidentifier.xyz/u/<your Discord ID> and find it in Users, together with the balls you collect and, only if you turn it on, a summary of your Ballsdex stats (never your API key or ball IDs)
Catch Stats On servers where an admin turned on Catch stats, the bot reads the dex bots' spawn and catch messages there and, for each catch, saves your Discord ID, the server ID, the dex and ball, how long the catch took (the time between the spawn message and the catch message) and when it happened. From these it keeps your totals: number of catches, average and fastest time, overall, per ball and per server. Your catch stats are private (only you see them) unless you turn on “Show my catch stats publicly”; then they appear on your profile and in the Catch stats rankings. You can stop being tracked at any time (“Don't track me” under a catch reply, /catchstats settings tracking:False or your profile), which deletes this data
Website Login (optional) When you log in on the website with Discord: an encrypted, HttpOnly session cookie with your Discord ID, username, avatar and a Discord access token used to list your servers. It is kept in your browser, not on our servers

Server/Guild Data: When the bot is used in a Discord server, we collect:

Data Type Description
Server ID Your Discord server's unique identifier
Notifier Configuration Selected bot IDs, role ID, custom message, setup user, and setup timestamp stored for the notifier managed on the website
Catch Stats Settings Whether Catch stats are on, the reply settings, who changed them and when, and the server's fastest catch
Guild Usage Counts Aggregated identification totals used for public bot statistics
Note: We do not permanently store raw spawn images. Attachments are processed temporarily to generate hashes, and the temporary file is deleted after processing.

3. Media Processing & Reports

When you use the identify command or notifier features, the bot downloads the attached spawn image, creates a temporary resized copy, computes an image hash, and then removes the temporary file.

If the match confidence is low or an error occurs, the bot may send a report to a configured webhook. Those reports can include your Discord tag or ID, the detected match, the original image URL, the bot involved, and error details.

4. Data Retention

User Data:

  • Your user statistics and notification preferences are stored until you ask us to remove them or stop using the service.
  • Your Ballsdex sign-in and synced collection data are kept until you unlink your account (on the website's Ballsdex tab or with /ballsdex unlink), which deletes them and revokes BallIdentifier's access. You can also remove that access on your Ballsdex Keys page.
  • The website session cookie expires after 7 days at most, or when you log out.
  • Your profile settings are kept until you ask us to remove them. Turning off Public profile hides your profile and removes it from Users right away.
  • Single catches (for the 7 and 30 day Catch stats) are deleted automatically after 35 days. Your catch totals, averages and records are kept until you turn off catch tracking, which deletes all of your catch data right away.

Server Data:

  • Notifier configuration is retained until it is removed on the website or deleted from storage.
  • Catch stats settings are kept until an admin changes them; turning Catch stats off stops new catches from being counted on that server.
  • Aggregated usage counters are kept for service statistics.

5. Where Data is Stored

Data is stored in the bot's asset files on our hosting infrastructure and in any configured third-party services used for bot operations, such as Discord Bot List and optional webhooks.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data only when necessary to run the service, including the following:

  • Discord Bot List for fetching upvote status and posting bot stats
  • The Ballsdex API, called with your own Ballsdex sign-in, only when you connect, sync or check your Ballsdex account (and, for the BallIdentifier owner's account, to load the public specials and market data)
  • Discord DMs for optional thanks messages and upvote reminders
  • Configured log, error, report or upvote webhooks
  • Hosting and storage infrastructure used to run the bot and website
